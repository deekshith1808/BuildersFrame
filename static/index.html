Matching & Guided Planning

Transparent scoring and template-guided plans

Instead of opaque AI claims, BuildersFrame uses a documented weighted formula to rank challenges against your team's current skills and provides template-guided starter roadmaps you can edit and export.

  • Explainable match formula: 30 baseline + up to 32 skill & alias overlap + up to 20 level fit + 12 domain alignment + team/hardware/stack checks.
  • Template-guided starter drawer: Generates domain-matched stack ideas, 5 user-interview prompts, and sprint milestones labeled clearly as starting points.
  • One-click Markdown brief: Export selected problems from My Stack into a structured document for team alignment.
